# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, project partners, recruiters, and fellow software engineers evaluating Patrick's experience, technical range, and working style. They need to understand his capabilities quickly, verify them through concrete work, and find a clear path to contact him.

## Product Purpose

Present Patrick Wozniak's professional work, technical expertise, writing, and open-source projects in a credible personal portfolio. Success means visitors can quickly identify relevant experience, gain confidence in his ability to deliver high-quality software, and decide to start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Technical, personal, and confident. The site should feel direct, experienced, thoughtful, and approachable rather than corporate or self-promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic agency templates, inflated marketing claims, visual gimmicks that compete with the work, anonymous corporate language, and portfolio pages that hide evidence behind decorative presentation.

## Design Principles

- Lead with concrete work and verifiable outcomes.
- Make technical breadth easy to scan without flattening the details.
- Communicate expert confidence through clarity, not hype.
- Keep Patrick's personal voice visible in copy and presentation.
- Provide equivalent, complete experiences in English and German.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain strong contrast, keyboard-accessible interactions, semantic structure, meaningful alternative text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential animation.
